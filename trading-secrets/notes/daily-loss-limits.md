# Daily Loss Limits

A daily loss limit is a predetermined cap on the amount of money a trader is willing to lose in a single trading day. It is a critical component of a robust [[risk-management]] strategy and a key element of a [[trading-plan]].

## Purpose

- **Capital Preservation**: Prevents a single bad day from causing significant damage to a trading account.
- **Emotional Discipline**: Forces a trader to stop trading when they are likely emotional and not in a good state to make decisions, thus preventing [[revenge-trading]] and [[overtrading]].
- **Enforces Discipline**: Adhering to a daily loss limit is a fundamental aspect of [[trading-discipline]].

## Setting Limits

A daily loss limit can be set as a fixed dollar amount or a percentage of the total account balance. For example, a trader might set a limit of not losing more than 1-2% of their account in one day.
