# Revenge Trading

Revenge trading is an emotionally driven behavior where a trader, after experiencing a loss, deviates from their [[trading-plan]] in an attempt to quickly recoup their losses. This often leads to impulsive decisions, larger position sizes, and taking on excessive [[risk-management]] in an irrational pursuit of "getting back" at the market.

## Causes

-   **Frustration and Anger**: Losing money can trigger strong negative emotions.
-   **Desire for Immediate Gratification**: The urge to recover losses quickly, rather than patiently waiting for high-probability setups.
-   **Lack of [[emotional-control]]**: Inability to manage psychological responses to losing trades.

## Consequences

-   **Increased Losses**: Revenge trades are typically unplanned and poorly executed, often leading to even larger losses.
-   **[[overtrading]]**: Taking too many trades without proper analysis.
-   **Erosion of [[trading-discipline]]**: Undermining the rules and principles of a well-defined [[trading-plan]].

Overcoming revenge trading requires strong [[emotional-control]], a disciplined approach to [[handling-losses]], and a commitment to sticking to one's predetermined [[risk-per-trade]] limits.
