## Summary
This note explains the anatomy of a candlestick and how to interpret the story it tells about price action within a specific timeframe.

## Anatomy of a Candlestick

```mermaid
graph TD
    A[Candlestick] --> B[Real Body];
    A --> C[Wicks / Shadows];
    B --> B1[Color (Green/Red)];
    B --> B2[Open & Close Prices];
    C --> C1[Upper Wick (High)];
    C --> C2[Lower Wick (Low)];
```
