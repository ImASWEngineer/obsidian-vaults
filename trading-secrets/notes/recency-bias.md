## Summary
This note explains [[recency-bias]], the tendency to place too much emphasis on recent experiences or data points when making decisions.

## Impact on Trading
- After a series of winning trades, a trader might become overconfident and take on excessive risk, believing their recent success will continue indefinitely.
- After a significant loss or a string of losses, a trader might become overly fearful and hesitate to take valid trade setups, as seen in [[overcoming-fear]].

## Mitigation Strategies
- Trust your data-driven strategy ([[momentum-day-trading-strategy-v1]]) over recent emotional highs or lows.
- Maintain a consistent approach to [[risk-management]] regardless of recent outcomes.
- Review long-term performance in your [[trading-journal]] to maintain perspective.
