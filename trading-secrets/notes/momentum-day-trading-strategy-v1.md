## Summary
This note defines the initial, data-driven [[momentum-day-trading-strategy-v1]] that Ross Cameron developed after hitting "rock bottom" and analyzing his past trades. It is a foundational strategy focused on high-probability setups.

## Core Components of the Strategy
- **Stock Selection**: Strictly trade stocks that meet the five criteria discovered through [[analyzing-your-own-trades]]. This is codified in [[guardrail-01-momentum-stock-criteria]].
- **Focus**: Trade small-cap stocks, avoiding large-caps (like Apple) and penny stocks (under $1).
- **Goal**: To find stocks with a fundamental imbalance of supply and demand, leading to predictable momentum.
- **Execution**: Wait for a stock to prove itself by moving, then trade the momentum. This is a "buy high, sell higher" approach, not "buy low, sell high."
