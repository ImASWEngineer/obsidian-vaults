## Summary
This note differentiates between the two main types of stock a company can issue: common stock and preferred stock.

## Key Differences
- **Common Stock**:
  - Represents ownership in a company.
  - Grants voting rights to shareholders.
  - Has the potential for significant capital appreciation, but dividends are not guaranteed.
  - Last in line to receive assets in the event of liquidation.
- **Preferred Stock**:
  - Typically does not grant voting rights.
  - Pays a fixed, regular dividend, making it more stable.
  - Has priority over common stockholders for receiving dividends and assets in a liquidation.
  - Often considered a hybrid between a stock and a bond.
