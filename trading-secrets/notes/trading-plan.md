# Trading Plan

A trading plan is a comprehensive set of rules and guidelines that a trader follows for every trade. It is a written document that covers all aspects of a trader's methodology, from market analysis to trade execution and risk management. A trading plan is essential for maintaining [[trading-discipline]] and achieving consistent results.

## Why a Trading Plan is Crucial

- **Removes Emotion**: It forces a trader to make decisions based on logic and a predefined strategy, rather than on [[fomo-in-trading]] or [[fear-of-losing]].
- **Provides Consistency**: Ensures that every trade is executed with the same set of rules, which is vital for performance analysis.
- **Builds Confidence**: Trading with a well-researched plan builds confidence and reduces stress.
- **Acts as a Roadmap**: Guides a trader through all market conditions.
