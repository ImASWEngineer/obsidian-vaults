## Summary
This note details the core software components that form a day trader's toolkit. The right software provides the information and execution speed needed to compete.

## The Software Stack
1.  **Brokerage Platform**: The software provided by your [[choosing-a-day-trading-broker|broker]] for order execution. It must be fast, reliable, and ideally support hotkeys for rapid order entry and cancellation.
2.  **Charting Platform**: The primary tool for [[technical-analysis-moc|technical analysis]]. It must provide high-quality, real-time data, advanced charting tools, indicators, and drawing capabilities. (e.g., TradingView, Thinkorswim).
3.  **Stock Scanner**: An indispensable tool for finding trading opportunities in real-time. Scanners search the entire market for stocks that meet a trader's specific criteria, such as those in [[guardrail-01-momentum-stock-criteria]].
