# Volume Analysis

Volume analysis is a component of [[technical-analysis]] that involves studying the number of shares or contracts traded in a security or market over a given period. Volume provides insight into the strength or weakness of a price movement.

## Key Principles

-   **Confirmation**: High volume confirms a price trend or pattern. For example, a strong breakout from a [[support-and-resistance]] level on high volume is more reliable than one on low volume.
-   **Divergence**: When price and volume move in opposite directions, it can signal a potential reversal. For instance, if a stock's price is rising but volume is declining, it suggests weakening buying interest.
-   **Exhaustion**: Extremely high volume after a prolonged trend can indicate a climax, suggesting the trend is about to reverse.

## Application

Volume is often analyzed in conjunction with price action, [[candlestick-patterns]], and other indicators like [[moving-averages]] or [[vwap]]. It helps traders gauge market conviction and identify potential turning points.

For example, a [[breakout-trading]] strategy might look for increased volume to confirm the validity of a price breaking out of a consolidation pattern.

Volume is a critical aspect of overall [[market-analysis]].
