# Technical Analysis

Technical analysis is a trading discipline employed to evaluate investments and identify trading opportunities by analyzing statistical trends gathered from trading activity, such as price movement and volume.

Unlike fundamental analysis, which attempts to evaluate a security's value based on business results, technical analysis focuses on charts of price movement and various analytical tools to evaluate a security's strength or weakness.

## Core Principles

1.  **Market Action Discounts Everything**: All known information is already reflected in the price.
2.  **Prices Move in Trends**: Prices tend to follow trends (up, down, or sideways).
3.  **History Tends to Repeat Itself**: Chart patterns are repetitive and predictable to a certain extent.

## Key Tools and Concepts

- **Charting**: The primary tool for technical analysts.
- **[[candlestick-patterns]]**: Visual representations of price movements.
- **[[support-and-resistance]]**: Key price levels where the price tends to stop and reverse.
- **[[trend-analysis]]**: Identifying the direction of the market.
- **[[volume-analysis]]**: Analyzing the number of shares traded to confirm trends.
- **Indicators**:
    - [[moving-averages]]
    - [[rsi]] (Relative Strength Index)
    - [[vwap]] (Volume-Weighted Average Price)
- **Patterns**:
    - [[breakout-patterns]]
    - [[reversal-patterns]]
    - [[continuation-patterns]]
