## Summary
This note explains the importance of a catalyst—a specific news event—in driving the significant price movement required for a [[momentum-day-trading-strategy-v1]].

## Types of Catalysts
- **Earnings Reports**: Positive or negative surprises in a company's quarterly earnings.
- **FDA Announcements**: Drug trial results or approvals for biotech companies.
- **Contracts and Partnerships**: News of a major new contract or strategic partnership.
- **Analyst Upgrades/Downgrades**: A significant change in rating from a reputable analyst.
