# Pattern Entries

Pattern entries involve initiating a trade based on the recognition and confirmation of specific chart patterns formed by price action. These patterns, identified through [[technical-analysis]], often suggest predictable future price movements, offering high-probability entry points.

## Types of Patterns for Entries

-   **[[candlestick-patterns]]**: Individual or combinations of candlesticks that signal potential reversals or continuations (e.g., Hammer, Engulfing, Doji for reversals; Three White Soldiers for continuation).
-   **[[breakout-patterns]]**: Formations that indicate a period of consolidation followed by a strong move out of a defined range (e.g., Rectangles, Triangles, Flags, Pennants). Entries are typically made as price breaks out of the pattern.
-   **[[reversal-patterns]]**: Chart formations that suggest a change in the prevailing trend (e.g., Head and Shoulders, Double Top/Bottom). Entries are made upon confirmation of the reversal.
-   **[[continuation-patterns]]**: Formations that indicate a temporary pause in an existing trend before it resumes (e.g., Flags, Pennants, Wedges). Entries are made as the price breaks out of the pattern in the direction of the original trend.

## Confirmation

Successful pattern entries often require confirmation from other indicators, such as increased [[volume-analysis]] on a breakout, or a shift in momentum indicators like [[rsi]]. A well-defined [[trading-plan]] will specify the exact criteria for validating a pattern entry.
