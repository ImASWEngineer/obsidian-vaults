## Summary
Anchoring bias is the tendency to rely too heavily on the first piece of information (the "anchor") offered when making decisions, even if that information is irrelevant or misleading.

## Impact on Trading
- **Price Anchors**: A trader might anchor to a stock's previous high price, believing it "must" return there, ignoring current market conditions.
- **Initial Analysis**: Over-reliance on an initial analysis or price target, even when new information suggests otherwise.

## Mitigation Strategies
- Always re-evaluate your trade thesis with fresh data.
- Focus on current price action and [[technical-analysis]] rather than past price levels as the sole determinant.
