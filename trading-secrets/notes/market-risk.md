# Market Risk

Market risk refers to the possibility of losses in a trading position due to factors that affect the overall performance of financial markets. These factors include changes in interest rates, exchange rates, equity prices, and commodity prices. Unlike specific risks (e.g., company-specific news), market risk affects a broad range of assets.

## Types of Market Risk

-   **Interest Rate Risk**: The risk that changes in interest rates will affect the value of a security.
-   **Equity Price Risk**: The risk that stock prices will decline.
-   **Currency Risk**: The risk that changes in exchange rates will affect the value of foreign investments.
-   **Commodity Price Risk**: The risk that changes in commodity prices will affect the value of investments in those commodities.

Managing [[market-risk]] is a key aspect of overall [[risk-management]], often involving diversification and hedging strategies. It is an inherent part of [[market-analysis]].
