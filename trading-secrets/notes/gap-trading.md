# Gap Trading

Gap trading is a [[day-trading-strategies]] that focuses on price gaps that occur when a stock's opening price is significantly higher or lower than its previous day's closing price, creating an empty space on the chart. These gaps are often caused by overnight news, earnings reports, or other significant market-moving events.

## Types of Gaps

-   **Common Gaps**: Small gaps that are usually filled quickly and have little significance.
-   **Breakaway Gaps**: Occur at the beginning of a new trend, often on high [[volume-analysis]], signaling strong conviction.
-   **Runaway Gaps (Continuation Gaps)**: Appear in the middle of a strong trend, indicating continued momentum.
-   **Exhaustion Gaps**: Occur near the end of a trend, often on high volume, signaling a final surge before a reversal.

## Strategy Overview

Gap trading strategies vary widely, from "fading" (trading against) gaps that are expected to fill, to "playing" (trading with) gaps that are expected to continue. Understanding the context of the gap (e.g., news, volume, previous trend) is crucial for determining the most probable outcome.

## Risk Management

Gaps can lead to high volatility, making [[risk-management]] paramount. [[stop-loss]] orders are essential, and position sizing should be adjusted to account for the increased risk.
