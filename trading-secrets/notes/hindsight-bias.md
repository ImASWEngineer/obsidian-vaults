## Summary
Hindsight bias, often called the "I knew it all along" effect, is the tendency to perceive past events as more predictable than they actually were.

## Impact on Trading
- **False Confidence**: Can lead to overconfidence in one's analytical abilities, making a trader believe they could have predicted a market move that was, in reality, unpredictable.
- **Poor Learning**: Hinders effective learning from mistakes because the trader might rationalize losses by saying they "should have known," rather than objectively analyzing their process.

## Mitigation Strategies
- Maintain a detailed [[trading-journal]] to record your thought process *before* a trade.
- Focus on process and probabilities, not just outcomes.
