## Summary
Drawdown management refers to the strategies and psychological approaches used to limit losses during periods of underperformance and to recover effectively. A drawdown is a peak-to-trough decline in an investment, account, or fund during a specific period.

## Key Principles
- **Acceptance**: Understand that drawdowns are an inevitable part of trading.
- **Early Recognition**: Identify when you are in a drawdown quickly.
- **Reduce Exposure**: The first step is often to reduce [[position-sizing]] or trading frequency.
- **Re-evaluation**: Use drawdowns as an opportunity to review your [[trading-journal]] and analyze your strategy. See [[analyzing-your-own-trades]].
- **"Trader Rehab"**: Implement strict self-imposed rules to stop the bleeding and rebuild confidence. See [[trader-rehab]].
