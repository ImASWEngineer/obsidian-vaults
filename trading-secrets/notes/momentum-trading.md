# Momentum Trading

Momentum trading is a [[day-trading-strategies]] that seeks to capitalize on the continuation of existing trends. Traders identify stocks that are moving strongly in one direction (up or down) on high [[volume-analysis]] and enter trades in the direction of that movement, expecting the momentum to continue.

## Key Characteristics

-   **Strong Price Movement**: Stocks exhibiting significant price changes over a short period.
-   **High Volume**: Sustained momentum is often confirmed by above-average trading volume, indicating strong interest from market participants.
-   **Catalyst**: Often, momentum is driven by a specific news event, earnings report, product launch, or sector-wide shift.

## Strategy Overview

Momentum traders typically look for stocks that have recently made new highs (for long trades) or new lows (for short trades) and are showing signs of continued strength. They use [[technical-analysis]] indicators like [[moving-averages]], [[rsi]], or [[vwap]] to confirm the trend and identify potential entry and exit points.

## Risk Management

Due to the fast-moving nature of momentum stocks, strict [[risk-management]] is crucial. Traders often use tight [[stop-loss]] orders and look for quick [[profit-targets]] as momentum can reverse rapidly. [[trailing-stops]] are also common to protect gains.
