# Pullback Entries

Pullback entries are a strategy used to enter a trade in the direction of the prevailing [[trend-analysis]] after a temporary counter-trend movement, known as a "pullback" or "retracement." This allows traders to get a more favorable entry price than chasing a stock that is already moving strongly.

## Identification

-   **Established Trend**: First, identify a clear uptrend or downtrend.
-   **Retracement Levels**: Look for the price to pull back to key levels such as:
    -   Previous [[support-and-resistance]] levels (where old resistance becomes new support in an uptrend, and vice-versa).
    -   Dynamic support/resistance provided by [[moving-averages]] (e.g., 20-period EMA, 50-period SMA).
    -   Fibonacci retracement levels.
-   **Confirmation**: Wait for signs that the pullback is ending and the original trend is resuming (e.g., a bullish [[candlestick-patterns]] at a support level in an uptrend).

## Execution

Traders enter a long position when the price bounces off a support level during an uptrend, or a short position when it rejects a resistance level during a downtrend. [[stop-loss]] orders are placed just beyond the pullback low/high to manage risk. This strategy is a core component of [[pullback-trading]].
