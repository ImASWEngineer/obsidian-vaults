## Summary
This note addresses the common question: "If you're so successful at trading, why do you teach?" It outlines Ross Cameron's motivations for teaching and building a trading community.

## Key Motivations
1.  **Inherent Passion for Teaching**: Ross comes from a family of teachers and has a natural inclination to teach, which he did even before trading (e.g., ceramics classes).
2.  **Improved Personal Trading**: Explaining trades out loud forces clarity of thought. The accountability from viewers and community members helps enforce discipline.
3.  **No "Secrets" to Guard**: Real day trading success comes from learning market dynamics and investor psychology, not from a secret formula. Sharing knowledge doesn't diminish his edge.
4.  **Building a Platform**: To provide the best tools (scanners, charts, chat), he hired a development team. The cost of maintaining this platform necessitates charging for the service, moving beyond just free content.
