# Handling Losses

Losses are an inevitable part of trading. How a trader handles these losses significantly impacts their long-term success and mental well-being. Effective loss management is a cornerstone of [[trading-psychology]] and directly influences [[trading-discipline]].

## Psychological Impact of Losses

-   **Emotional Hijack**: Losses can trigger strong emotions like frustration, anger, fear, and regret, leading to impulsive decisions. See [[emotional-hijack]].
-   **Revenge Trading**: The urge to immediately recoup losses, often by taking larger, unplanned risks. See [[revenge-trading]].
-   **Fear of Losing**: Past losses can create a [[fear-of-losing]] that leads to hesitation, missed opportunities, or premature exits from profitable trades.

## Strategies for Handling Losses

-   **Acceptance**: Acknowledge that losses are part of the business and not personal failures.
-   **Strict [[risk-management]]**: Define and adhere to [[risk-per-trade]] and [[daily-loss-limits]] to ensure no single loss is catastrophic.
-   **Post-Trade Analysis**: Objectively review losing trades to learn from mistakes without emotional bias. Focus on process, not outcome.
-   **Emotional Detachment**: Separate your self-worth from your trading performance.
-   **Breaks**: Step away from the screens after a significant loss to regain composure and objectivity.
