## Summary
This note explains [[confirmation-bias]], which is the tendency for traders to search for, interpret, favor, and recall information that confirms their pre-existing beliefs or hypotheses about a trade.

## Impact on Trading
- A trader who is bullish on a stock will actively seek out positive news and bullish technical patterns while ignoring or downplaying bearish signals.
- This leads to poor [[risk-management]], as the trader fails to see the full picture and may not exit a losing position when their initial thesis is proven wrong.

## Mitigation Strategies
- Actively seek out disconfirming evidence. Ask yourself, "What is the bear case for this trade?"
- Adhere strictly to a [[trading-plan]] with predefined exit criteria.
- Use a [[trading-journal]] to objectively review why trades failed.
