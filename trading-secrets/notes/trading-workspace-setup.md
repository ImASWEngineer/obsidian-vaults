## Summary
This note describes how to set up an efficient and distraction-free trading workspace. A professional environment fosters a professional mindset and enhances focus and discipline.

## Key Elements of an Optimal Workspace
- **Dedicated and Quiet Space**: A separate room or area used exclusively for trading. This minimizes distractions from family, pets, or television and helps create a psychological boundary between trading and personal life.
- **Ergonomics**: An ergonomic chair and a desk at the proper height are essential to prevent physical strain and maintain focus during long market hours. Monitors should be positioned at eye level.
- **Organization and Cleanliness**: A clean, organized desk reduces mental clutter. Keep only essential items within reach.
- **Eliminate Distractions**: This is a critical part of [[trading-discipline]]. Turn off all non-essential notifications on your phone and computer. Close unnecessary browser tabs. Treat trading like the serious business it is.
