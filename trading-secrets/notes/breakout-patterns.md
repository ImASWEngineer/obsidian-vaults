# Breakout Patterns

Breakout patterns are specific formations on price charts that signal a potential move out of a defined trading range. They are a key component of [[technical-analysis]] and are central to [[breakout-trading]] strategies. These patterns indicate periods of consolidation where supply and demand are in balance, followed by a decisive move that breaks this equilibrium.

## Common Breakout Patterns

-   **Rectangles (Trading Ranges)**: Price moves horizontally between clear [[support-and-resistance]] levels. A breakout occurs when price moves decisively above resistance or below support.
-   **Triangles (Ascending, Descending, Symmetrical)**: Formed by converging trendlines.
    -   *Ascending Triangle*: Flat resistance, rising support. Bullish breakout expected.
    -   *Descending Triangle*: Flat support, falling resistance. Bearish breakout expected.
    -   *Symmetrical Triangle*: Converging support and resistance. Breakout direction is less predictable.
-   **Flags and Pennants**: Short-term continuation patterns that form after a sharp price move. They represent a brief pause before the trend resumes.

## Confirmation

A valid breakout from these patterns is typically confirmed by a significant increase in [[volume-analysis]], indicating strong buying or selling pressure. Traders look for [[breakout-entries]] as the price clears the pattern's boundaries.
