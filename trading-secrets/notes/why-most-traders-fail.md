## Summary
This note outlines the primary reasons why the vast majority of aspiring day traders fail to achieve consistent profitability. Success is not about finding a secret indicator, but about overcoming these common pitfalls.

## The Cycle of Failure

```mermaid
graph TD
    A[1. Lack of Education] --> B[2. Unrealistic Expectations];
    B --> C[3. Poor [[risk-management]]];
    C --> D[4. Emotional Trading<br>(Fear, Greed, [[revenge-trading]])];
    D --> E[5. Significant Losses];
    E --> F{Blows Up Account / Quits};
    D --> C;

    subgraph "Common Pitfalls"
        direction LR
        A & B & C & D
    end
```

## Key Reasons for Failure
1.  **Lack of Proper Education**: Treating trading like gambling instead of a business.
2.  **Poor Risk Management**: Risking too much per trade, not using stop losses, and not having a plan. See [[risk-management-MOC]].
3.  **Emotional and Psychological Weakness**: Succumbing to fear, greed, FOMO, and revenge trading. See [[trading-psychology-moc]].
4.  **Lack of a Proven Strategy**: Trading without a statistical edge. See [[momentum-day-trading-strategy-v1]].
5.  **Impatience and Inconsistency**: Not sticking to the plan or giving the strategy enough time to work.
