# Breakout Entries

Breakout entries are a common type of entry signal used in [[breakout-trading]] strategies. They occur when the price of an asset moves decisively above a resistance level or below a support level, signaling a potential continuation of the new trend.

## Identification

-   **Key Levels**: Identifying strong [[support-and-resistance]] levels that have held multiple times.
-   **[[breakout-patterns]]**: Recognizing chart patterns like rectangles, triangles, or flags that indicate consolidation before a potential breakout.
-   **[[volume-analysis]]**: A valid breakout is typically accompanied by a significant increase in trading volume, confirming strong conviction behind the move.

## Execution

Traders typically enter a long position when the price breaks above resistance, or a short position when it breaks below support. A [[stop-loss]] is usually placed just inside the broken level to manage [[risk-per-trade]]. The expectation is that the breakout will lead to a sustained move in the direction of the break.
