## What is a Stock?
A stock (also known as equity) represents a share of ownership in a publicly-traded company. When you buy a company's stock, you are buying a small piece of that company.

Companies issue stock primarily to raise capital to fund operations, expansion, or new projects. This is a form of [[equity-vs-debt-financing]].
