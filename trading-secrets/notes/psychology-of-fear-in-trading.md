## Summary
This note explores the different types of fear that traders experience and how they can negatively impact performance.

## The Two Primary Fears
1.  **Fear of Losing Money**: This is the most obvious fear. It can cause traders to hesitate on valid setups, cut winners short to lock in small profits, or move stop-losses further down, violating their [[risk-management]] plan.
2.  **Fear of Missing Out (FOMO)**: This fear drives traders to chase stocks that are already extended, enter trades without a proper setup, or jump back into the market after a good day, often leading to giving back profits. See [[fomo-in-trading]].

## Overcoming Fear
- Acknowledge that losses are a part of trading. Focus on the process, not the outcome of a single trade.
- Have a well-defined [[trading-plan]] and trust it.
- Reduce [[position-sizing]] when feeling fearful to lower the stakes.
