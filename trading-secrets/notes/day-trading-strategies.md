# Day Trading Strategies

Day trading strategies are systematic methods for buying and selling securities within the same trading day. A successful strategy is built upon a solid foundation of [[technical-analysis]] and strict [[risk-management]] rules, all of which should be documented in a [[trading-plan]].

## Common Day Trading Strategies

Below are links to common strategies used by day traders. Each strategy has its own specific entry and exit criteria.

- **[[breakout-trading]]**: Trading the move that occurs when a stock's price moves outside a defined support or resistance level.
- **[[momentum-trading]]**: Riding the "wave" of a stock that is making a strong move in one direction on high volume.
- **[[pullback-trading]]**: Entering a position after a stock has temporarily pulled back from its primary trend.
- **[[gap-trading]]**: Capitalizing on the price difference between the previous day's close and the current day's open.

Effective execution of any strategy requires [[trading-discipline]].
