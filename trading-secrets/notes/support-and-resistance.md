# Support and Resistance

Support and resistance are key concepts in [[technical-analysis]] that represent price levels on a chart where the price of an asset tends to stop and reverse. These levels are denoted by horizontal lines.

- **Support**: A price level where a downtrend can be expected to pause due to a concentration of demand. As the price drops towards support, it becomes cheaper, and buyers become more inclined to buy, "supporting" the price.

- **Resistance**: A price level where an uptrend can be expected to pause due to a concentration of supply. As the price rises towards resistance, sellers become more inclined to sell, creating a price "ceiling".

## Visual Representation (ASCII)

The price moves between support and resistance levels, bouncing off them.

```
     / \   / \
    /   \ /   \
---/-----\/-----\---  <- Resistance Level
  /       \   /
 /         \ /

---/\---------/\----  <- Support Level
   \/         \/
```

## Identification

Support and resistance levels can be identified by looking at historical price action, specifically previous peaks (resistance) and troughs (support). Other indicators like [[moving-averages]] and [[trend-analysis]] can also act as dynamic support or resistance.

## Trading Application

These levels are crucial for determining entry and exit points for trades. A break above resistance can signal a [[breakout-trading]] opportunity, while a bounce from support can be an entry for a [[pullback-trading]] strategy. They are also logical places to set a [[stop-loss]].
