## Summary
This note outlines the key factors to consider when selecting a broker for active day trading. The right broker is a critical component of a trader's toolkit.

## Key Factors to Consider
1.  **Execution Speed & Routing**: How quickly are orders filled? Direct-access brokers often provide faster, more reliable fills than those who sell order flow.
2.  **Commissions and Fees**: Understand the cost structure (e.g., per-share vs. per-trade). High fees can significantly erode profitability.
3.  **Platform Stability**: The trading software must be robust and reliable, especially during volatile market conditions. Frequent crashes are unacceptable.
4.  **Shorting Availability (Easy-to-Borrow List)**: A good inventory of stocks available to short is crucial for traders who play both sides of the market.
5.  **Customer Support**: Responsive and knowledgeable support is vital when technical or trade-related issues arise.
6.  **Account Minimums & Rules**: Be aware of account minimums, especially concerning the [[pattern-day-trader-rule]].
