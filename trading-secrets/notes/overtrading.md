# Overtrading

Overtrading is the act of trading too frequently or with excessive position sizes, often deviating from a well-defined [[trading-plan]]. It is a common psychological pitfall that can lead to significant losses and erode [[trading-discipline]].

## Causes of Overtrading

-   **Boredom**: Trading out of a need for action rather than valid setups.
-   **[[revenge-trading]]**: Attempting to quickly recoup losses after a bad trade.
-   **[[fomo-in-trading]]**: Fear of missing out on perceived opportunities.
-   **Lack of Patience**: Inability to wait for high-probability setups.
-   **Overconfidence**: Believing one can consistently beat the market.

## Consequences

-   **Increased Transaction Costs**: Frequent trading incurs higher commissions and fees.
-   **Higher Exposure to Risk**: More trades mean more opportunities for losses, especially if setups are low quality.
-   **Emotional Exhaustion**: Constant trading can lead to burnout and poor decision-making.
-   **Deviation from Plan**: Overtrading often involves ignoring [[risk-management]] rules and entry/exit criteria.

Combating overtrading requires strong [[emotional-control]], strict adherence to a [[trading-plan]], and a focus on quality over quantity of trades.
