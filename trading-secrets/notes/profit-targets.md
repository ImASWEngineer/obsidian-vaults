# Profit Targets

A profit target is a predetermined price level at which a trader plans to exit a profitable trade to realize gains. Setting clear profit targets is a crucial component of a well-defined [[trading-plan]] and effective [[risk-management]].

## Purpose

-   **Discipline**: Helps to remove emotion from decision-making, preventing greed from leading to holding winning trades too long, which can result in giving back profits.
-   **Risk-Reward Management**: Ensures that trades align with a favorable [[risk-to-reward-ratio]], as the potential profit is defined before entry.
-   **Consistency**: Promotes consistent execution of a trading strategy.

## Methods for Setting Profit Targets

-   **Fixed Price Levels**: Based on previous [[support-and-resistance]] levels, chart patterns, or a fixed dollar amount.
-   **Percentage Gain**: Exiting when the price has moved a certain percentage in your favor.
-   **Technical Indicators**: Using indicators like [[moving-averages]], [[rsi]], or [[vwap]] to signal overbought conditions or exhaustion of momentum.
-   **Volatility-Based**: Setting targets based on the average true range (ATR) or other volatility measures.

Profit targets work in conjunction with [[stop-loss]] orders to manage the full spectrum of trade outcomes.
