## Summary
[[trading-psychology]] refers to the mental and emotional aspects that influence trading decisions. Ross Cameron emphasizes that controlling emotions and maintaining discipline are often more important than the trading strategy itself.

## Key Psychological Challenges
1. **Fear and Greed**
   - [[fear-of-missing-out-fomo]]
   - [[fear-of-losing]]
   - [[overtrading]] due to greed

2. **Emotional Control**
   - Managing losses without going on tilt ([[emotional-control]])
   - Avoiding [[revenge-trading]]
   - Maintaining composure during [[drawdowns]]

3. **Discipline**
   - Sticking to the [[trading-plan]]
   - Following [[risk-management]] rules
   - Avoiding impulsive decisions

## Psychological Tools and Techniques
- [[trading-journal]] - Journaling trades to identify emotional patterns.
- Meditation and mindfulness practices.
- Setting realistic expectations.
- Taking breaks to avoid mental fatigue.
- Celebrating small wins.

## Common Psychological Pitfalls
- [[confirmation-bias]]
- Overconfidence after wins (related to [[hindsight-bias]])
- [[loss-aversion]]
- Analysis paralysis (can be mitigated by a clear [[trading-plan]])
