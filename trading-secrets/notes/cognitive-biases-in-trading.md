## Summary
This note serves as a hub for understanding the common cognitive biases that negatively impact a trader's decision-making process. Recognizing and mitigating these biases is a cornerstone of developing [[trading-discipline]].

## Common Biases
- [[confirmation-bias]]: The tendency to favor information that confirms pre-existing beliefs.
- [[loss-aversion]]: The tendency to prefer avoiding losses to acquiring equivalent gains.
- [[recency-bias]]: Giving too much weight to the most recent information or trades.
- [[anchoring-bias]]: Over-relying on the first piece of information offered (the "anchor") when making decisions.
- [[hindsight-bias]]: The "I knew it all along" effect, which can lead to overconfidence.
