## Summary
This note details the first and most fundamental guardrail in Ross Cameron's system: the specific criteria for identifying strong momentum stocks to trade. This forms the core of his stock scanning and selection process.

## The 5 Criteria
1.  **Up over 10% on the day** versus the previous day's closing price.
2.  **High relative volume** (at least 2.0 times the average).
3.  **Share price between $2 and $10**, and not more than $20.
4.  **A news catalyst** announced today.
5.  **Relatively low float** (preferably 20 million shares or fewer).
