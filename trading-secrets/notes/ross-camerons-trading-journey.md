## Summary
This note serves as a hub for understanding the key phases and turning points in [[ross-camerons-trading-journey]] to becoming a successful day trader. His path was marked by significant personal and financial challenges, which ultimately shaped his disciplined approach.

## Key Phases of the Journey

1.  **Early Influences**: A school stock market project and a friend's penny stock success sparked his initial interest.
2.  **Initial Struggles**: Faced anxiety, personal challenges, and the pressure to make a living, leading him to day trading out of necessity.
3.  **The "Penny Stock" Phase**: Followed "hot tip" newsletters, leading to frustration and dependency.
4.  **The PDT Hurdle**: Encountered the [[pattern-day-trader-rule]] and had to fund his account to $25,000.
5.  **Two Steps Forward, Three Steps Back**: Experienced a cycle of small wins followed by large, emotionally-driven losses.
6.  **Hitting Rock Bottom**: A significant loss dropped his account below the PDT minimum, forcing him to sell personal assets and re-evaluate everything.
7.  **The Turning Point**: Conducted a deep, data-driven review of his trade history, leading to the discovery of his core strategy. See [[analyzing-your-own-trades]].
8.  **Developing Discipline**: Implemented strict rules, including the mantra to [[get-green-and-shut-it-down]] and entering [[trader-rehab]] when necessary.
9.  **Building Consistency**: Focused on his new [[momentum-day-trading-strategy-v1]] and slowly climbed back to profitability.
10. **The Teacher Arc**: Started sharing his journey and knowledge, which led to the creation of his trading community. See [[why-successful-traders-teach]].
11. **Proof of Concept**: Undertook the [[583-to-10-million-challenge]] to validate his strategy from a small account.
