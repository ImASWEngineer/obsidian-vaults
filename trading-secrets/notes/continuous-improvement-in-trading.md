## Summary
This note outlines the key practices for continuous improvement, a requirement for long-term success in trading.

## Key Practices
1.  **Trade Journaling**: Meticulously record every trade, including the setup, execution, and outcome. See [[trading-journal]].
2.  **Regular Review**: Set aside time daily or weekly to review your trades. Identify what worked, what didn't, and why. This is the core loop of [[analyzing-your-own-trades]].
3.  **Adaptation**: The market is always changing. A successful trader must be willing to adapt their strategies based on performance and changing market conditions.
