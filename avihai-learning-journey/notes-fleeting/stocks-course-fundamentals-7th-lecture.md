        Finviz is highlighted as one of the best and most popular stock screeners available, free to use with a comprehensive set of features.

    What is a Stock Screener?
        A screener is a tool used to subset the market to identify stocks that meet predefined criteria, essential because the entire stock universe (~7,000+ symbols) is too large to analyze fully.
        By applying filters, traders can focus on a smaller, manageable list of stocks tailored to their strategy.

    Using Finviz for Fundamental (Value) Investing
        Example 1: A fundamental value investor seeking low PE (Price-to-Earnings) stocks to find undervalued companies.
        Initial universe: 7,000 stocks → applying “Low PE” filter reduces to ~850 stocks.
        Adding high dividend yield filter (to find stable, reliable companies) narrows list to 173.
        Adding minimum average daily volume (e.g., 300,000 shares) further reduces to 60 stocks.
        Geographic filter applied to limit to U.S. companies, yielding 48 stocks.
        Sector filter added (e.g., financial sector) narrows the list to 38 stocks.
        This stepwise filtering process helps tailor the universe for a conservative, value-driven portfolio.

    Key Finviz Data Tabs and Metrics
        Valuation Tab: PE, forward PE, EPS, etc.
        Financial Tab: Dividends, ROE (Return on Equity), ROI (Return on Investment), debt-to-equity ratios, etc.
        Ownership Tab: Float, insider ownership, institutional ownership, short float.
        Technical Tab: Quick chart views and trend analysis.
        Custom Tab: Available to Elite members (~$30-$40/month), allows custom data fields and exporting data to Excel (CSV) for further analysis.

    Using Finviz for Momentum (Growth) Trading
        Example 2: Momentum trader looking for growth stocks with high PE (>40) and no dividends (typical of reinvesting growth companies).
        Sector filter applied to Healthcare, a volatile sector with explosive potential.
        Technical filter applied to find stocks at 52-week highs (indicating momentum breakout).
        Resulting list: only 6 stocks fitting this momentum profile.
        This refined list forms a watchlist for potential buying opportunities with tight stop losses.

    Additional Finviz Features
        Alerts can be set for stocks meeting specific criteria, but this requires Elite membership. Alerts notify users via email when conditions (e.g., reaching 52-week high) are met.
        The free version is sufficient for most screening purposes without needing to export data or set alerts.

    Summary of Finviz Advantages
        Free, comprehensive, easy to use.
        Supports both fundamental and technical filters.
        Allows creation of watchlists tailored to different trading styles.
        Elite membership adds data export and alerts features but is optional.
        Highly recommended for traders needing to filter large universes efficiently.
