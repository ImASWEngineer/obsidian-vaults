# Continuation Patterns

Continuation patterns are chart formations in [[technical-analysis]] that suggest a temporary pause or consolidation in an existing [[trend-analysis]], after which the original trend is likely to resume.

## Common Continuation Patterns

-   **Flags and Pennants**: Short-term patterns that form after a sharp, strong price move, representing a brief period of consolidation before the trend continues.
-   **Triangles (Symmetrical, Ascending, Descending)**: While triangles can be reversal patterns, they often act as continuation patterns, with the price breaking out in the direction of the prevailing trend.
-   **Rectangles**: A trading range where price moves between parallel [[support-and-resistance]] levels before continuing the prior trend.

## Confirmation

A continuation is confirmed when the price breaks out of the pattern in the direction of the original trend, ideally on increased [[volume-analysis]]. These patterns provide opportunities for [[pattern-entries]] to join an established trend.
