## Summary
This note explains the crucial skill of achieving [[emotional-detachment-in-trading]]. It means executing trades based on a predefined strategy and rules, rather than being influenced by fear, greed, hope, or ego.

## The Goal: Trade Like a Robot
- **Execute the Plan**: The primary job of a trader during market hours is to execute their [[trading-plan]] flawlessly. The analysis and decision-making should happen *before* the market opens.
- **Remove Subjectivity**: Emotional detachment removes the subjective feelings that lead to common errors like [[revenge-trading]], holding losers too long, or cutting winners too short.
- **Acknowledge Indifference**: True detachment comes from accepting that [[the-market-is-always-right]]. The market does not know or care about your positions or personal situation.

## Practical Steps
1.  **Have a Plan**: Never enter a trade without a clear entry, exit, and stop-loss strategy.
2.  **Use Hard Stops**: Implement non-negotiable stop-losses to protect capital.
3.  **Journal Trades**: Review trades after the market closes to analyze performance objectively, as detailed in [[analyzing-your-own-trades]].
