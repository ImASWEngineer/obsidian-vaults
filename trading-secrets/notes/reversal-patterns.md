# Reversal Patterns

Reversal patterns are chart formations in [[technical-analysis]] that signal a potential change in the current [[trend-analysis]]. These patterns indicate that the prevailing trend is losing momentum and may be about to reverse direction.

## Common Reversal Patterns

-   **[[head-and-shoulders-pattern]]**: A common pattern signaling a top (Head and Shoulders) or bottom (Inverse) reversal.
-   **Double Top / Double Bottom**: Indicates a potential trend change after failing to break through a key [[support-and-resistance]] level twice.
-   **Rounding Top / Bottom**: A gradual change in trend direction, forming a bowl or dome shape.

## Confirmation

A reversal is typically confirmed when the price breaks a key trendline or support/resistance level, often accompanied by an increase in [[volume-analysis]]. Traders use these patterns to time [[pattern-entries]] for the new, emerging trend.
