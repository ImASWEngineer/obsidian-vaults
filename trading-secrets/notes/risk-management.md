# Risk Management

Risk management is the cornerstone of successful trading. It is the process of identifying, assessing, and controlling threats to a trader's capital and profitability. Without a solid risk management strategy, even a profitable trading system can lead to ruin.

## Core Principles

- **Capital Preservation**: The primary goal is to protect your trading capital from significant losses.
- **Emotional Discipline**: Having predefined risk rules helps to mitigate the impact of [[fear-of-losing]] and greed, preventing [[revenge-trading]].
- **Longevity**: Proper risk management ensures a trader can survive losing streaks and remain in the game long-term.
