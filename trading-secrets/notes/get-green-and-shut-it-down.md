## Summary
This note explains the mantra "Get green and shut it down," a key disciplinary rule Ross Cameron adopted to regain control of his trading and build consistency, especially after a drawdown.

## The Mantra Explained
- **Purpose**: To prevent giving back profits and to avoid over-trading, particularly when confidence is low or the market is choppy.
- **Execution**: Trade within a limited time window (e.g., two hours). Once a profitable trade makes you "green" for the day, stop trading. Shut down the computer to remove temptation.
- **Context**: This is a crucial strategy for beginners, traders in a slump, or anyone in "[[trader-rehab]]". It builds confidence and discipline by focusing on small, consistent wins.
