# Trading Discipline

Trading discipline is the ability to consistently follow your [[trading-plan]] and adhere to your risk management rules, regardless of emotional impulses. It is a critical component of [[trading-psychology]] and often separates successful traders from unsuccessful ones.

## Key Aspects of Discipline

- **Sticking to Your Plan**: Executing trades only when they meet the predefined criteria in your trading plan.
- **Emotional Control**: Maintaining [[emotional-control]] and not letting fear or greed dictate your actions. This includes avoiding [[revenge-trading]] and [[overtrading]].
- **Patience**: Waiting for high-probability setups and not forcing trades out of boredom or [[fear-of-missing-out-fomo]].
- **Consistent Execution**: Applying your strategy and risk rules consistently over time, even after a series of [[handling-losses]].

## Common Discipline Challenges

- [[overcoming-fear]]: Specifically, the [[fear-of-losing]] money.
- [[fomo-in-trading]]: The fear of missing out on a big move.
- [[emotional-hijack]]: When emotions take over rational decision-making.

Developing a [[growth-mindset]] is essential for building and maintaining trading discipline.
