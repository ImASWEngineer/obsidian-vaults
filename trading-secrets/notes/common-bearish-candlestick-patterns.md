## Summary
This note covers common bearish candlestick patterns that suggest a potential reversal of an uptrend or continuation of a downtrend.

## Key Bearish Patterns
- **Shooting Star**: A bearish reversal pattern with a long upper wick, little or no lower wick, and a small real body at the low of the day.
- **Bearish Engulfing**: A large red candle that completely engulfs the previous green candle, indicating strong selling pressure.
- **Evening Star**: A three-candle bearish reversal pattern, consisting of a large green candle, a small-bodied candle (or doji), and a large red candle.
