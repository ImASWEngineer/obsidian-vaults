## Summary
This note outlines the criteria Ross Cameron believes are essential for a credible and effective day trading teacher, emphasizing experience, transparency, and a willingness to share both successes and failures.

## The 6+1 Criteria
1.  **Has personally done it**: The teacher must be an active or experienced trader.
2.  **Has traded for years**: Longevity demonstrates consistency beyond a lucky streak.
3.  **Has been consistently successful**: The teacher should have a proven track record of profitability.
4.  **Shares successes and failures**: A willingness to discuss losses is a sign of honesty and provides crucial learning opportunities.
5.  **Can document their track record**: This means providing audited brokerage statements, not just cherry-picked screenshots.
6.  **Is willing to teach**: Not all successful traders are willing or able to teach effectively.
7.  **(Bonus) Didn't have an easy time**: A teacher who struggled and overcame obstacles is often more relatable and better at explaining the process than a natural prodigy.
