## Summary
This note provides a consolidated checklist of the key trading rules or "guardrails" from the book. This serves as a quick reference to reinforce [[trading-discipline]].

## The Guardrails
1.  **Stock Selection**: Only trade stocks that meet the strict criteria in [[guardrail-01-momentum-stock-criteria]].
2.  **Risk Management**: Never risk more than a predefined percentage of your account on a single trade.
3.  **Daily Loss Limit**: Stop trading for the day if you hit your maximum daily loss.
4.  **Discipline Mantra**: [[get-green-and-shut-it-down]], especially when in [[trader-rehab]].
