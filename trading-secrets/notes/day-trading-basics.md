## Summary
Day trading involves buying and selling financial instruments within the same trading day. Ross Cameron emphasizes the importance of understanding market mechanics, order types, and developing a solid trading plan before risking real capital.

## Key Points
- Definition: Holding positions for minutes to hours, closing all positions by market close
- Key markets: Stocks, forex, futures, and cryptocurrencies
- Market hours: Understanding pre-market, regular, and after-hours trading
- Order types: Market, limit, stop-loss, and OCO (One-Cancels-Other) orders
- Risk management: Never risk more than 1-2% of capital on a single trade
- Importance of paper trading before using real money

## Common Mistakes
- Overtrading
- Revenge trading
- Ignoring stop-loss orders
- Letting emotions drive decisions
