## Summary
This note covers common bullish candlestick patterns that suggest a potential reversal of a downtrend or continuation of an uptrend.

## Key Bullish Patterns
- **Hammer**: A bullish reversal pattern that forms after a decline, featuring a long lower wick and a small real body at the top of the range.
- **Bullish Engulfing**: A large green candle that completely engulfs the previous red candle, indicating strong buying pressure.
- **Morning Star**: A three-candle bullish reversal pattern, consisting of a large red candle, a small-bodied candle (or doji), and a large green candle.
