## Summary
This note describes [[loss-aversion]], a cognitive bias where the psychological pain of losing is about twice as powerful as the pleasure of gaining.

## Impact on Trading
- **Holding Losers Too Long**: Traders avoid realizing a loss by holding onto a losing position, hoping it will turn around. This turns a small, manageable loss into a catastrophic one.
- **Cutting Winners Too Short**: Traders are quick to take small profits to lock in a "win," fearing the profitable trade will turn against them. This damages the [[risk-reward-ratio]].

## Mitigation Strategies
- Use hard [[stop-loss-strategies]] to mechanically define your maximum acceptable loss.
- Define profit targets in your [[trading-plan]] to let winners run.
