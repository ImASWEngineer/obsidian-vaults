## Summary
This note explains the concept of High Relative Volume (RVOL) and why it's a critical indicator for momentum traders.

## What is Relative Volume?
Relative Volume compares the current trading volume to the average volume over a past period (e.g., 30 days). An RVOL of 2.0 means the stock is trading at twice its normal volume for that time of day.

- High RVOL is a key part of [[guardrail-01-momentum-stock-criteria]] because it signifies unusual interest in a stock, often driven by a [[catalyst-for-stock-movement]]. This institutional interest is what fuels strong, sustained momentum.
