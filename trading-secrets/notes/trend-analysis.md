# Trend Analysis

Trend analysis is a core component of [[technical-analysis]] that involves identifying the direction of the market or a specific asset's price movement over a period. The basic premise is that prices tend to move in trends, and understanding these trends can help predict future price movements.

## Types of Trends

-   **Uptrend (Bullish)**: Characterized by a series of higher highs and higher lows.
-   **Downtrend (Bearish)**: Characterized by a series of lower lows and lower highs.
-   **Sideways/Consolidation (Range-bound)**: Prices move within a relatively narrow range without a clear upward or downward direction.


## Tools for Trend Analysis

-   **Trendlines**: Lines drawn on a chart connecting a series of highs or lows to visualize the trend.
-   **[[moving-averages]]**: Often used to smooth out price data and identify the underlying trend.
-   **Chart Patterns**: [[continuation-patterns]] and [[reversal-patterns]] can indicate the likely continuation or reversal of a trend.

Traders use trend analysis to align their trades with the prevailing market direction, aiming to "trade with the trend."
