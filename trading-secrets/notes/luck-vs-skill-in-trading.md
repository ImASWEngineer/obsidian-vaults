## Summary
This note explores the relationship between luck and skill in day trading. While any single trade can be influenced by luck, consistent profitability over the long term is a product of skill and a statistical edge.

## The Role of Luck
- **Short-Term Randomness**: In the short term, market movements can be random. A beginner can get lucky on a few trades, and an expert can have a losing streak.
- **The "Lucky" Beginner**: Early success due to luck can be dangerous, as it often leads to overconfidence and poor [[risk-management]].

## The Role of Skill
- **Statistical Edge**: Skill is the ability to identify and execute a strategy that has a positive expected value over many trades. This is the "edge". See [[momentum-day-trading-strategy-v1]].
- **Consistency**: Skill is demonstrated through consistent application of a strategy and disciplined risk management, not through winning every trade.
- **Data-Driven Approach**: A skilled trader relies on a data-driven approach, like the one developed in [[analyzing-your-own-trades]], rather than hoping for luck.
